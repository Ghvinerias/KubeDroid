# Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 9, 2026

## Overview

KubeDroid is designed with a local-first privacy model. The app does not collect personal data, does not use analytics trackers, and does not send your Kubernetes credentials to any third-party service.

## Data Collection

KubeDroid does not collect, store, or sell personal data.

KubeDroid does not collect:
- Name, email, phone number, or address
- Location data
- Analytics or usage tracking events
- Advertising identifiers
- Crash reports sent to external services

## Credentials and Cluster Data

Kubernetes credentials (including kubeconfig contexts, tokens, certificates, and related authentication material) are stored locally on your device.

KubeDroid uses these credentials only to connect directly to the Kubernetes API endpoints you configure.

Credentials are not transmitted to KubeDroid-operated servers because KubeDroid does not run a backend service for credential processing.

## Network Use

Any network traffic generated by KubeDroid is limited to:
- Direct communication between your device and your configured Kubernetes clusters
- Standard Android platform networking required for that direct communication

KubeDroid does not send data to analytics platforms, ad networks, or telemetry backends.

## Data Sharing

KubeDroid does not share user data with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because data is stored locally, you control retention.

You can remove app data at any time by:
- Deleting configured contexts inside the app (if available), and/or
- Clearing app storage in Android system settings, and/or
- Uninstalling the app

## Children’s Privacy

KubeDroid is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, updates will be published in this file and distributed with app updates.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository.
