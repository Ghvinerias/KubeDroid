KubeDroid brings Kubernetes operations to Android with a fast, touch-friendly workflow inspired by terminal tools.

Manage production and development clusters from your phone or tablet without carrying a laptop.

What you can do with KubeDroid:
- Connect to one or more clusters using kubeconfig contexts
- Browse namespaces and resources with responsive, native UI screens
- Inspect pod health, status, labels, annotations, and events
- Stream pod logs and quickly troubleshoot failures on the go
- Open remote exec sessions for shell-level debugging
- View deployments and monitor rollout state
- Inspect nodes and key cluster infrastructure details
- Work with common Kubernetes objects from one mobile app

Built for real-world operations:
- Native Android app built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
- Smooth navigation optimized for both phones and larger screens
- Modern architecture for reliability and maintainability
- No account required to start using your own clusters

Privacy and security first:
- No analytics, no telemetry, no ad SDKs
- No Kubernetes credentials are sent to external servers
- Credentials and configuration stay on your device

KubeDroid is designed for SREs, platform engineers, and developers who need practical Kubernetes visibility and control anywhere.
